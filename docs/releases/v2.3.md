*Smooth 60fps seekbar, smart audio categorization (songs vs call recordings vs ringtones), gallery photo swipe & sleek top bar, Settings category swiping, and AllRounder Media Player branding.*

### What's new in v2.3

- **Image Viewer & Gallery Screen Refinements:**
  - Removed circular dark background circles from top bar buttons to eliminate overlap and provide a sleek, modern look.
  - Removed the bottom hint overlay text ("Pinch to zoom • Double tap to zoom • Swipe to change") for an unobstructed full-screen viewing experience.
  - Fixed horizontal swiping between photos in `HorizontalPager` while preserving smooth pinch-to-zoom and double-tap zoom.
- **Buttery Smooth 60fps / 120fps Music Progress Bar:**
  - High-frequency frame clock with `withFrameMillis` provides fluid progress tracking without stutter or stepped 250ms jumps.
  - Seeking is completely lag-free: scrubbing updates local UI at 120fps with zero player IPC overhead, committing the seek only when the finger lifts.
- **Smart Audio Categorization (Songs vs Call Recordings vs Ringtones):**
  - Audio files are intelligently categorized based on MediaStore path, folder, filename, and duration patterns.
  - Pure songs are isolated from recorded phone calls, voice memos, and short ringtones.
  - Dedicated category pills for `[ Recordings ]` and `[ Ringtones ]` in Music view so users can access them whenever needed without polluting their music library, shuffle, or quick picks.
  - The "FEATURED PICK" hero card only selects from pure songs (never ringtones or call recordings).
- **Auto-Refreshing Featured Pick:**
  - Switching to the Music tab from Video or MYCinema automatically selects a fresh new featured song recommendation every time.
- **Video Tab Section Reordering:**
  - Swapped "CONTINUE WATCHING" and "RECENTLY ADDED" sections, moving "RECENTLY ADDED" to the top.
- **Settings Screen Category Swiping & App Branding:**
  - Upgraded Settings screen with `HorizontalPager` so users can swipe horizontally across categories (`All`, `Playback`, `Gestures`, `Subtitles`, `Appearance`, `Library`, `Music`, `Storage`), automatically synchronizing with the top category navigation chips.
  - Updated all app name references to **AllRounder Media Player**.
