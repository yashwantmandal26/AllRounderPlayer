### What's new

- **Sleek & Non-Colliding Volume / Brightness Sliders:** Slimmed down music vertical gesture bars (14dp capsule with 6dp edge padding) to guarantee zero overlap with tonearm, vinyl record platter, and favorite buttons. Added dedicated floating percentage pill.
- **Continuous Seamless Progress Bar:** Removed all awkward gaps around the seekbar circle thumb (`thumbTrackGapSize = 0.dp`) and removed end stop indicator dot. Refined sleek 3.0dp track with dynamic 4.5dp interactive scrub expansion and glowing thumb knob.
- **App Startup Update Check:** The app automatically checks for new releases on startup (once per app launch session) and immediately notifies the user with the built-in update window and Android status bar notification. Users can download & install, skip version, or choose not now.
- **Removed Guide Screens:** Eliminated first-time tutorial/walkthrough overlays across Home, Music Player, and Video Player screens for a faster, clean, uninterrupted experience.
- **TMDb Universal & Deep Linking:** Open TMDb movie and TV series links directly into MYCinema details with poster gallery sharing and downloads.
