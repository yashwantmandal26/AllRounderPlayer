*Direct download sites (HDHub4u & KatMovieHD), instant torrent streaming buffer, offline artist avatars, enhanced ambient glow, and UI polish.*

### What's new in v2.7

- **Direct Download Sites Integration (HDHub4u & KatMovieHD):**
  - Integrated one-tap access to leading movie and series download portals directly from the MyCinema media details modal.
  - Automatic dynamic domain resolution and fallback health checks via `DownloadSitesManager`.
  - Immersive in-app browsing using dark-themed Chrome Custom Tabs with site accent branding.
- **Instant Torrent Streaming & Real-Time Buffering:**
  - Sequential piece prioritization engine: streams play within seconds by prioritizing container header (moov atom) and initial chunks.
  - Redesigned `StreamBufferingDialog` displaying live download speeds, connected peer/seed counts, progress percentage, and responsive cancellation.
- **Torrent Sources Bottom Sheet Polish:**
  - Modern bottom sheet with clear badges for resolution (4K HDR, 1080p, 720p), video codec, file sizes, and seeders.
  - Quick action chips to stream instantly or download to local storage in the background.
- **Offline Artist Portrait Caching:**
  - High-resolution artist portraits are automatically fetched and cached locally on first load, with seamless fallback to embedded track artwork.
  - Distinct circular artist badges throughout the Music library and mini player.
- **Video Player Ambient Lighting Overhaul:**
  - High-fidelity ambient backdrop with configurable saturation (up to 2.32x) and smooth Gaussian spread.
  - Edge-to-edge video immersion with seamless aspect ratio clamping and gesture controls.
- **Audio Categorization & Playback Fixes:**
  - Polished separation of pure music from call recordings and ringtones.
  - Smoother mini player pill animation and playback service stability.
