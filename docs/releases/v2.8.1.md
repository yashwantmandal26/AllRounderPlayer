*Video Player Top Options Toolbar, Background Touch Gesture Isolation, Songs Deduplication, Three-Finger Screenshot Stability & Queue Playback Fixes.*

### What's New in v2.8.1

- **Sleek Horizontal Options Toolbar in Video Player:**
  - Redesigned the 3-dots options menu into an ultra-clean, top-aligned horizontal scrollable toolbar.
  - Matches the circular control styling of Subtitles and Audio buttons with theme accent borders, dark radial gradients, and compact labels.
  - Instant access to all 13 player tools: Autoplay, Sleep Timer, Night Mode, Ambient Mode, Dynamic Theme, Equalizer, Subtitles, A-B Repeat Loop, Bookmarks, Media Info, Share, Delete, and Settings.

- **Background Touch Gesture & Scrubber Isolation:**
  - Resolved progress bar dragging conflict: scrolling horizontally through the options toolbar no longer triggers video seeking.
  - Added touch zone guarding and child consumption protection to separate player swipe gestures (seek, volume, brightness) from UI controls.

- **Smart Multi-Layer Music Deduplication:**
  - Filters out duplicate songs across storage folders (e.g., `Download/` vs `Music/`), duplicate MediaStore IDs, and 0-byte ghost files.
  - Automatically preserves the highest quality track with complete tags and album metadata.

- **Gesture & Playback Stability:**
  - Fixed 3-finger screenshot freeze by properly cleaning up pointer states on focus loss and gesture release.
  - Fixed crash when selecting currently playing song for "Play Next" or Queue with safe feedback.
  - Synchronized Queue reordering directly with Media3 timeline.
  - Restored pre-fade audio volume when cancelling or extending sleep timers.
  - Fixed queue auto-loop when Repeat is turned Off.
  - Preserved video pause state during timeline scrubbing.
  - Video sleep timer now persists across screen orientation changes.
  - Enhanced contrast on white-accent buttons.
